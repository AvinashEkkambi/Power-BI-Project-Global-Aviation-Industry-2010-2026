Global Aviation Industry Dashboard – Power BI Project Analysis

This Power BI project presents an interactive analysis of the Global Aviation Industry from 2010 to 2026, focusing on airline performance, revenue generation, operating margins, fleet size, and regional comparisons. The dashboard is divided into two pages, providing both a summarized overview and a detailed analysis of the aviation sector.

Page 1: Global Aviation Industry Overview

The first dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the industry's key performance indicators (KPIs), including total revenue of 7.81K BN, operating margin of 1.62K, total income of 363.84 BN, and fleet size of 38.80K. Interactive filters allow users to explore business models such as legacy, low-cost, and regional airlines. Year and region slicers enable focused analysis across different periods and geographical areas.

Various visualizations highlight airline performance and market trends. The top 10 airlines by operating margin, regional revenue distribution, and top five passenger-carrying airlines help identify major industry players. Additional charts compare operating margins with load factors and fleet sizes, while other visualizations identify airlines generating maximum revenue and compare fleet size against passenger capacity.

Page 2: Detailed Airline and Operating Cost Analysis

The second page provides a detailed tabular view of airline performance from 2010 to 2018. It displays airline-wise yearly financial figures, allowing users to compare performance across different years. The table also includes a total row for understanding overall financial trends.

The bottom section presents a bar chart showing maximum operating costs across different airlines, categorized by geographical regions such as Asia, Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, and North America. This visualization helps identify airlines with higher operating expenses and compare their cost structures.
